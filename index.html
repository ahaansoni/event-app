How it runs

Four stages, no surprises

  1. 01

    Enquiry & date hold

    You share the date, headcount and a rough budget. We hold the date for seven days at no cost while you decide.

  2. 02

    Proposal & site visit

    A written scope with line-item costing, two or three venue options, and a walkthrough of the shortlist with you.

  3. 03

    Build-up

    Vendor contracts, tastings, sample decor setup, and a shared timeline you can check any day of the week.

  4. 04

    Event days

    Our team is on site from the first truck to the final teardown. One named person is your only phone number.

Questions

Asked often enough to answer here

How far ahead should we book?

For a November–February wedding, six to nine months. Corporate events need four to six weeks minimum. If your date is closer than that, call rather than fill the form — we can sometimes make it work.

What does the advance look like?

Thirty per cent on signing, forty at the start of build-up, the balance on the final event day. Venue and catering advances are paid directly by you to the property, so nothing sits with us.

Do you work outside Jaipur?

Yes — Udaipur, Jodhpur, Pushkar and Ranthambore regularly, and elsewhere in India for existing clients. Travel and stay for the team is billed at cost.

Can we use our own caterer or decorator?

Of course. We'll fold them into the timeline and coordinate them like our own vendors. We don't take commissions from vendors, so we have no reason to push you toward ours.

What happens if it rains?

Every outdoor plan we write has a wet-weather version attached, decided at a cut-off time we agree with you in advance — usually the morning of. No panic calls at 6pm.

Do you give GST invoices?

Yes, on every engagement, corporate or personal. Purchase orders and vendor onboarding paperwork are handled by our accounts team.
